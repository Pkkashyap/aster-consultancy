About us

Your one-stop consultancy partner.

Aster Consultancy Services is a trusted consultancy dedicated to empowering educational institutions and organisations through expert guidance: education accreditation, professional recruitment, healthcare and business certifications, digital marketing, AI and automation, environmental and sustainability services, and compliance support.

We are committed to professionalism, integrity and client success. With a customer-centric approach and industry expertise, we deliver practical, result-oriented solutions that help institutions and organisations grow, while creating meaningful career opportunities for individuals.

Our mission

To provide reliable consultancy, compliance, accreditation and recruitment services that enable institutions and organisations to achieve sustainable growth, operational excellence and long-term success.

Our vision

To become one of India's most trusted consultancy firms by delivering innovative, ethical and high-quality solutions in education, healthcare and recruitment.

Ask a question
Can we hire Aster for just one service?

Yes. Engagement is flexible: you can commission a single service, such as GST filing or NAAC consultancy, or a cross-disciplinary combination coordinated by one point of contact.

Do you work outside Noida and Varanasi?

Yes. We deliver services across India, both online and offline. Our offices are in Noida and Varanasi.

How does NAAC accreditation consultancy work?

We provide end-to-end consultancy for colleges and universities, covering quality assurance, regulatory compliance and the certifications required by statutory and accreditation bodies. Contact us to discuss your institution's requirements and timeline.

What is TPA empanelment for hospitals?

TPA (Third-Party Administrator) empanelment connects a hospital with insurance companies and healthcare networks. We assist hospitals through the empanelment process and with certifications such as NABH, FSDA and PC-PNDT.

Do job seekers pay to register?

No. Registration is free for candidates. We match you to openings across IT, BFSI, healthcare, FMCG, manufacturing, sales and other sectors.

Is our information kept confidential?

Yes. We follow consistent quality, confidentiality and professionalism standards across every service and our partner network.
