Ask a question
Do candidates have to pay anything?

No. Registration and placement are completely free for candidates. Our fees are paid by employers.

How quickly can you send a shortlist?

It depends on the role and location. We reply to every enquiry within one working day with a realistic timeline, and urgent or time-critical roles are prioritised.

How do you verify candidates?

We check qualifications, professional registrations (for doctors and nurses), work experience and references before a candidate reaches you. Background checks are available on request.

What if a hire doesn't work out?

We offer replacement support if a placed candidate leaves early. The replacement terms are agreed with you before we start.

Can you manage payroll for contract staff?

Yes. Contract staff can be placed on our payroll, and we take care of salaries, statutory deductions and compliance so you get one simple monthly invoice.

Which locations do you recruit for?

Tell us where the role is based. We'll tell you honestly whether we can fill it and how long it's likely to take.
