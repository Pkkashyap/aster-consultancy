Aster Consultancy Services website.

Static site (plain HTML). Deployed on Render as a Static Site: build command empty, publish directory `.`

To update contact details, edit the phone number, email and address in index.html. They appear in the top bar, the Contact section and the footer, so find and replace all of them. The WhatsApp buttons use the phone number from the Contact section automatically.

Photos are in `assets/img/` and come from Pexels (free for commercial use, no attribution required): photos 5738735, 31499386, 7581117, 7580638 and 4930705 on pexels.com. Replace them with real photos of your team and office whenever you can.
